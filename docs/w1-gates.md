# W1 gates

Written 2026-10-21 by the W1 gatekeeper. Sources: `W0_CODE_MASTER_ELDER_VERDICT.md` section 5 (W1 entry gates) and
section 6 (LOW carry-forwards); `DESIGN_COUNCIL_MASTER_ELDER_VERDICT.md` section 5 (ME-22..ME-29, and the W0 gates
ME-15..ME-21 that every wave keeps honouring) and section 6 (LOW carry-forwards tagged W1); design section 10 W1;
`docs/w0-integration.md` "Left for W1". Both verdicts live in the vault under `projects/_staging/lj4evr/delegations/`.

Owner is the W1 track that lands the row: model | fixlib | engine | replay | sacn | actions | desktop | controls-a/b/c |
windows-a/b/c | app | integrator. Where the brief does not say which controls or windows sub-track owns a form, the
row names the form and the integrator assigns the letter. Shared files (`CMakeLists.txt` tests section,
`tests/lj_tests.c`, `tools/glyphmap.tsv`) are append-only for every track. The W1 code-pass council ticks each row
before `v0.1.0` is tagged; a row with no evidence is not ticked.

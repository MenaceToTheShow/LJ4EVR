## 0. Rules for this file

- **Times carried.** This counts the verdict carry lists the row has been in, W5 s6 included. A 3 means third or
  later. The W5 verdict s6 rule applies: a row at 3 must LAND in W6 or be CLOSED IN WRITING here. A close is a sentence
  in the Status cell saying why the row will not be done, and the council must accept it. A close is allowed only
  where the fix needs Martin assets we do not hold, the reference rig, or Joey.
- **Mode.** LAND means the fix and its Verify line land in W6. CLOSE-IN-WRITING follows the rule above. CARRY (Joey)
  covers the three Joey-only rows: they stay carried and are not track work. DONE means the row is already met at
  `bae0441`, and the Verify output is quoted.
- **Verify.** A row is ticked only when its Verify line has been run and its output quoted in
  `docs/w6-integration.md`. A claim does not tick a row.
- **Order (W5 verdict s1, process finding).** The orchestrator does not start a W6 track until the gates commit named
  on the first line of `docs/w6-contracts.md` exists in `git log`.
- **Standing.** These hold on every W6 commit:
  - every listener stays OFF on a fresh userdata;
  - no new third-party dependency;
  - OS-specific code stays in its `OS_ALLOW` file;
  - every network, serial, file or audio input is bounded;
  - no Martin byte enters git (the sha1 scan against `reference/`);
  - the REL-PRIVATE scan of `docs/w4-integration.md` ME-148 stays at 0 over the diff, the messages and the captures;
  - CI is GitHub-hosted only;
  - no force-push.
- **Owner tracks:** `net`, `dmxpatch`, `engine`, `hardening`, `fidelity`, and `Joey`.

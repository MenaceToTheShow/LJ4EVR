Sequences

A sequence is a list of scenes. A scene stores the values of the
channels set to snap or fade, a fade time and a scene time. When the
sequence runs, each scene fades in over its fade time and holds until
its scene time has passed, then the next scene starts.

The Sequence window (View > Controls > Sequence Control) shows the
current scene as two numbers, for example 2:5 for scene 2 of 5. Use its
buttons to add, insert, delete and step through scenes, and to set the
fade and scene times. Times are in seconds with hundredths.

Save Sequence stores the sequence in the user library with a number and
a name. A sequence can be a chase: then the scenes follow each other at
the chase speed instead of their own times.

Loop options decide what happens at the last scene: loop, bounce, stop,
or link to another cue. Sequence fades let one fade time apply to a
whole sequence.

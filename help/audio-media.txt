Audio Analyzer and MediaPlayer

Audio Analyzer
Turn it on in Setup > Hardware Setup, page Audio, and choose the input
device. The &Audio menu then appears; Start Audio Analyzer opens the
analyzer window. It listens to the input, finds beats and sends audio
trigs to every cue slot set to Audio trig mode. Audio-Trigs / Trig in
the cue says how many beats move a sequence on by one scene.
The faders set the decay (the shortest time between trigs), the
sensitivity and the noise cut-off; Use Equalizer limits the trigs to
the frequency bands you raise. Emulate Audio Trig (a hotkey or a DMX-in
function) sends a trig by hand.
Pass Keypresses to LightJockey: off, a hotkey pressed while the
analyzer has focus stays with the analyzer; on, it reaches LightJockey.
On macOS the system asks once for permission to use the microphone.

MediaPlayer
The cue list's MP rows control a built-in player: MP Load File, MP
Play, MP Seek, MP Stop, MP Pause, Wait for MP at, MP Close Clip, MP
Start and MP Close. Click the MediaPlayer time-code button in the cue
list to open the player and to show its clock.
Only WAV files play, up to 64 MB (about 6 minutes of CD-quality
stereo). The player has no video. A file name that points at another
computer, at a device or at anything but a .wav file is refused, and a
name without a folder is read from the library folder.

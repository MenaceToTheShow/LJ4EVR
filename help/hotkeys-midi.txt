Hotkeys and MIDI

Setup > Hotkeys > Edit Hotkeys assigns keys to functions: go, stop, load
cue, flash sequence, masters, and many more. Eight hotkey setups can be
stored and switched. Setup > Hotkeys > Hotkeys Enabled turns all hotkeys
on or off; the status bar shows when they are off.

MIDI In (from the C-L toolbar) maps MIDI notes and controllers to the
same functions. Press Learn, move the control on your MIDI device, and
the mapping is made. MIDI time code can drive the waits of a cue list.

The 2532 Direct Access window and the Virtual Fingers window give the
layouts of those Martin panels on screen. Their keys and faders can be
reached from the keyboard and from MIDI.

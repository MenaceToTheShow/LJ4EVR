Cues

A cue plays up to twelve sequences at once, one in each slot. The Cue
window lists the slots with the sequence number, name, scene counter,
times, status and loop mode. Slot 12 has the highest priority and slot 1
the lowest when two slots set the same channel.

Add a sequence to the current cue from the Sequence window or the list
of sequences. Save Cue stores the cue with a number and a name; the
window caption then shows that number and name. Go starts the cue, Stop
halts it, and the trigger buttons step the slots by hand.

Cue pages group cues for quick access. The Time Control window scales
the times of the whole cue or of single slots, from 1 to 999 percent of
what was programmed.

A cue can be transparent: it then loads on top of the running cue
instead of replacing it. Flash sequences play only while their key or
button is held, unless latching is turned on.

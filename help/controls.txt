Fixture controls

Select a fixture on the desktop and the fixture toolbar enables the
control windows that its profile has. Typical windows are Intensity,
Movement, Colors, Gobos, Beam, Effects, Lamp and Levels. Some fixtures
add an Extended window with settings and a reset button.

Every channel row has three buttons: O for off (the channel is not
stored in the scene), S for snap and F for fade. A snapped channel jumps
to its new value at the start of the scene; a faded channel moves to it
over the fade time.

The Movement window has a grid for pan and tilt. Drag inside the grid,
or use the arrow buttons for fine steps. Pan and tilt keep their full
16-bit resolution where the fixture has it. Locks, absolute or relative
mode, and the resolution setting change how the grid moves the
selection.

Colors and Gobos show a palette of tiles for each wheel. Click a tile to
send that slot. Wheel spin, rotation and shake have their own faders.

Changes go to all selected fixtures. The master fixture is the one whose
values the windows show.

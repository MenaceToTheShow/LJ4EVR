Not available in LJ4EVR

Since version 1.1.0 LJ4EVR includes the Audio Analyzer and audio
triggers, DMX input over sACN and Art-Net with the value tables, the
RS-232 and TCP remote, string output to external applications, the
importer for .LJB backups, the LJ MediaPlayer and Art-Net output.

What is still greyed, with the hint "Not available in LJ4EVR":

- the audio CD and Winamp rows of the cue list and of Hardware Setup;
- the video options of the MediaPlayer: it plays WAV sound files only;
- the per-channel "Use / Ignore DMX In Enable" items of the Global
  Patch.

The Martin hardware that LightJockey 2 drove directly (the 2510 and 2518
controllers, the MC-X, the CD control, SandNet and the USB interfaces)
is replaced by network output, so those items are greyed for good. The
help is English only; the other languages of the original help are not
included.

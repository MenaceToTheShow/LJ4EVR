Not available in LJ4EVR

This version does not include:

- the Audio Analyzer and audio triggers;
- DMX input over sACN, and the DMX-in value tables;
- the RS-232 and TCP remote protocol;
- string output to external applications;
- the importer for .LJB files;
- the LJ MediaPlayer;
- Art-Net output.

They are planned for a later version. Their menu items, toolbar buttons
and cue list commands are still there, greyed, with the hint "Not
available in LJ4EVR". The DMX In windows open so you can see their
settings, but their input controls stay greyed.

The Martin hardware that LightJockey 2 drove directly (the 2510 and 2518
controllers, the MC-X, the CD control, SandNet and the USB interfaces)
is replaced by network output, so those items are greyed for good. The
help is English only; the other languages of the original help are not
included.

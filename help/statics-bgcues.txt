Background cues and statics

A background cue runs under the current cue. It is useful for a base
look that stays while cues change on top. Open it from the C-L toolbar
or View > Controls > Background Cue. The toolbar light shows when a
background cue is active.

Statics hold fixed values that override what the cues send. The Statics
window is paged: each page has slots, and each slot stores the values of
some fixtures. Turn a slot on to apply it. A slot can add to, replace or
solo against the other slots, and slots can be grouped so that only one
of a group is on at a time.

The statics light on the toolbar is green while any static is active.

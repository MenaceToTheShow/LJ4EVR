LJ4EVR Help

This is the help for LJ4EVR, a lighting control program that works the
way LightJockey 2 did and sends its output as sACN (E1.31) over the
network. Pick a topic from the list on the left.

- Getting started: install, first run, the user library.
- The main window: menus, toolbars, the status bar and the fixture
  desktop.
- Fixtures: adding fixtures, addresses, profiles and the Unassigned
  list.
- Fixture controls: intensity, movement, colours, gobos, beam and the
  rest.
- Sequences: scenes, fades and saving.
- Cues: twelve sequence slots played together.
- Cue lists: running a show line by line.
- Background cues and statics: layers under and over the cue.
- Macros: movement macros and generic macros.
- Output: sACN, Art-Net, masters, blackout, freeze and offline.
- DMX-in and remote control: DMX from the network, the TCP and serial
  remote, External Application.
- Audio Analyzer and MediaPlayer: audio trig and WAV playback.
- Importing a LightJockey 2 backup: .LJB files.
- Hotkeys and MIDI: keyboard and MIDI control, 2532 and Fingers.
- Files and libraries: where your show lives, backups and restore data.
- Not available in LJ4EVR: what this version leaves out.
- Notices and licence: third-party software and the trademark note.

Every window keeps the menus and captions you know from LightJockey 2.
Items that belong to a feature this version does not have are still
shown, greyed, with the hint "Not available in LJ4EVR".
